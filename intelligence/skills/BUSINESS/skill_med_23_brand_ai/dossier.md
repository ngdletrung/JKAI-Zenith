## QUY TRINH THUC THI (7 BUOC)

### Buoc 1 — Brand Audit Hien Tai (Current State Assessment)
- Thu thap tat ca tai lieu brand hien co: logo (cac bien the), bang mau, font chu, anh chup man hinh.
- Danh gia tren 5 tieu chi: Visual Consistency, Tone of Voice, Audience Perception,
  Competitive Positioning, Digital Footprint.
- Dung tool: Semrush Brand Monitoring, Google Alerts, Social Mention de do luong cam nhan hien tai.
- Ket qua: Brand Health Score (1-10) cho tung tieu chi, danh sach diem manh va diem can cai thien.

### Buoc 2 — Xac Dinh Brand Persona (Nhan Cach Thuong Hieu)
- Neu thuong hieu la mot con nguoi: bao nhieu tuoi, nghe nghiep, gia tri song, cach noi chuyen?
- Dung Brand Archetype Framework (Jung): Hero, Sage, Jester, Caregiver, Creator... chon 1 chinh + 1 phu.
- Dinh nghia 3-5 tinh tu co lot ("Brand Adjectives"): vi du "chuyen nghiep, am ap, doi moi, minh bach".
- Viet trang Brand Persona 1 trang: ten gia dinh, anh dai dien, mo ta tinh cach, cau thuong noi.

### Buoc 3 — Xay Dung Brand Voice Guide (Huong Dan Giong Noi)
- Dinh nghia Tone of Voice tren 4 truc: Formal vs Casual / Serious vs Playful /
  Technical vs Simple / Respectful vs Bold.
- Viet "Do / Don't" cho 5 tinh huong giao tiep: post mang xa hoi, email, quang cao, xu ly khieu nai, PR.
- Vi du cu the moi loai: "Vi du DUNG: 'Chung toi biet ban dang bo. Hay de chung toi giup.'
  Vi du SAI: 'Quy khach vui long lien he hotline de duoc ho tro.'"
- Bao gom huong dan su dung xung ho (toi/ban, minh/cac ban, chung toi/quy vi).

### Buoc 4 — Tao Bo Guideline Mau Sac va Typography
- Primary palette: 1-2 mau chinh (hex code, RGB, CMYK, Pantone neu co in an).
- Secondary palette: 2-3 mau ho tro cho background, accent, CTA button.
- Typography: font chinh cho heading (bold, noi bat), font phu cho body text (de doc, tu nhien).
  Viet ro: font size hien thi tren web (H1/H2/H3/body), line height, letter spacing.
- Dark mode vs Light mode variation: mau nao doi, mau nao giu nguyen.
- Cung cap file guideline PDF + Figma link de doi ngu thiet ke tham chieu.

### Buoc 5 — Viet Brand Story (Cau Chuyen Thuong Hieu)
- Cau truc Brand Story theo mo hinh: TRUOC (van de ton tai) -> NGUYEN NHAN THANH LAP ->
  SU MENH (tai sao ton tai) -> TAM NHIN (muon di dau) -> LI DO KHACH HANG TIN TUONG.
- Do dai: 150-200 tu cho phien ban ngan (social), 400-600 tu cho phien ban day du (About page).
- Moi cau trong Brand Story phai phuc vu chuc nang: gai van de, khai sang, tao ket noi cam xuc.
- Kiem tra: doc Brand Story cho 3 nguoi la ngoai nganh, neu ho khong hieu -> viet lai.

### Buoc 6 — Tao Bo Mau Content Chuan Thuong Hieu (Content Templates)
- Thiet ke 5-10 template cho cac dinh dang thuong dung: Post vuong Instagram, Story, Banner web,
  Email header, Slide PowerPoint, Cover Facebook.
- Moi template phai su dung dung mau sac, font, logo theo Buoc 3 va Buoc 4.
- Tao "mau noi dung" cho tung loai post: giao duc, quang cao, testimonial, announcement.
- Luu file duoi dang Canva Brand Kit hoac Figma Component Library de toan doi su dung.

### Buoc 7 — Theo Doi Brand Consistency Tren Moi Kenh
- Thuc hien Brand Audit dinh ky: 1 lan/thang trong 3 thang dau, sau do 1 lan/quy.
- Checklist kiem tra moi kenh: Website, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Email, Tai lieu in.
- Do Brand Consistency Score: tinh % noi dung tuan thu guideline / tong noi dung kiem tra.
- Khi phat hien vi pham: cap nhat ngay, ghi nhan vao Brand Violation Log, dao tao lai nguoi tao.
