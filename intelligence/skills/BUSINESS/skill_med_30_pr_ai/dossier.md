## KICH HOAT KHI

- Nguoi dung yeu cau viet thong cao bao chi (Press Release) cong bo san pham, event, vong goi von moi.
- Phat hien cac tin don that thiet, tin tuc tieu cuc (Scandal/Crisis) dang lan truyen tren mang xa hoi va bao chi.
- Can soan thao thong tin phan hoi nhanh (Holding Statement) trong luc chờ xac minh su viec.
- Thiet lap moi quan he media (Media Outreach) va len ke hoach digital PR dai han.
- Nguoi dung dung cum tu: "viet thong cao bao chi", "xu ly khung hoang truyen thong", "dinh chinh tin don", "phat ngon bao chi".

## QUY TRINH CHI TIET

### BUOC 1 - AUDIT & DETECT: Quet va nhan dien tin tuc
- Su dung social listening tools (vi du: Buzzmetrics, SocialHeat) de do tim cac tu khoa tieu cuc va phan tich sentiment (thai do cua cong dong: Tich cuc / Trung lap / Tieu cuc).
- Xac dinh muc do anh huong cua tin tuc:
  - Level 1 (Nhe): Tin xau tren trang ca nhan cua nguoi dung it follower.
  - Level 2 (Vua): Bai viet tren cac group lon hoac blog cong nghe co tuong tac.
  - Level 3 (Nghiem trong): Tin tieu cuc len trang nhat cac bao dien tu lon (e.g., VnExpress, CafeF).

### BUOC 2 - CRISIS PROTOCOL: Kich hoat quy trinh xu ly trong gio vang
- Gio vang 1: Thanh lap ban chi dao khan cap (CEO, Legal, PR Director). Dung dang tai bat ky bai viet mang tinh tu ve hay giai thich ca nhan tren MXH.
- Gio vang 4: Soan thao Holding Statement (Phat ngon tam thoi) de xac nhan doanh nghiep da biet su viec va dang tien hanh xac minh thong tin.
- Gio vang 24: Dua ra thong bao chinh thuc (Official Statement) cung giai phap khac phuc triet de va thong diep xin loi chan thanh (neu loi thuoc ve doanh nghiep).

### BUOC 3 - STATEMENT WRITING: Soan thao thong cao phat ngon
- Soan theo nguyen tac 5W1H (Who, What, When, Where, Why, How) trong doan dau tien cua thong cao.
- Ap dung van phong trang trong, trung tinh, tap trung vao su that va du lieu, tranh dung tu ngu cam xuc hay bao bien.
- Trinh bay ro rang trich dan phat ngon (Quotes) tu nguoi dai dien phap luat hoac CEO.
- Dat dinh dang thong bao theo AP Style (Associated Press Stylebook).

### BUOC 4 - DARK SITE SETUP: Chuan bi san trang web an
- Thiet ke san mot landing page an (Dark Site) chua tat ca thong tin dinh chinh, bang chung phap ly va thong bao ve su viec.
- Chi kich hoat (Publish) dark site nay khi khung hoang bung no de lam kenh phat ngon duy nhat, tranh tinh trang tin tuc bi cat xén va tam sao that ban.

### BUOC 5 - MEDIA OUTREACH: Phat hanh va lam viec voi bao chi
- Gui truc tiep Press Release den danh sach nha bao (Media Contact List) phu trach dung linh vuc (e.g., Tech, Business, News).
- Su dung cac cong cu phan phoi thong cao bao chi nhu PR Newswire hoac cac dich vu booking PR cua local agency.
- Theo doi pickup rate (ty le bao dang lai) va lien he ho tro dính chinh neu cac bao dang sai lech thong tin goc.

### BUOC 6 - SOCIAL AMPLIFICATION: Dieu phoi du luan
- Phiet lap luong comment va tuong tac tren cac kenh truyen thong so huu cua doanh nghiep (Fanpage, Group).
- Phối hop voi `SOCIAL_DOM` de seeding cac thong tin tich cuc, cac bang chung khach quan de tu can bang lai luong du luan.

### BUOC 7 - POST-CRISIS RESTORATION: Phuc hoi sau khung hoang
- Tuyen bo ket thuc su co kem theo bang chung khac phuc hau qua thuc te.
- Chay cac chien dich PR tich cuc (Corporate Social Responsibility - CSR, giai thuong, thanh tuu cong nghe) de lay lai niem tin cua khach hang.
- Update quy trinh bao mat, quan ly rui ro he thong de ngan ngua lap lai.
