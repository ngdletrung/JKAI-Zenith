## QUY TRINH THUC THI (7 BUOC)

### Buoc 1 — Phan Tich Muc Tieu Video (Goal Analysis)
- Xac dinh ro muc tieu: Giao duc (brand authority), Ban hang (product demo/testimonial),
  Giai tri (brand awareness), hoac Thu hut lead (lead gen).
- Moi muc tieu co cau truc script va CTA khac nhau; khong mix 2 muc tieu trong 1 video.
- Xac dinh KPI do luong thanh cong: Views, Watch Time, CTR, Conversion, hoac Follower Growth.
- Ghi ro: nen tang chinh (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), do dai muc tieu (15s/30s/60s/90s).

### Buoc 2 — Viet Script Theo Cau Truc Hook-Content-CTA
- Hook (0-3 giay): cau hoi gay to mo, tuyen bo bat ngo, hoac visual an tuong.
  Vi du: "Ban dang lam sai dieu nay moi ngay ma khong hay."
- Content (60-80% do dai video): giai quyet van de, cung cap gia tri, dem vidu cu the.
  Moi diem noi dung toi da 1-2 cau, gap gon, khong luu loat.
- CTA (10 giay cuoi): huong dan cu the (comment tu khoa, nhan link, follow, mua ngay).
  CTA phai la hanh dong don gian, mot buoc, ro rang.
- Kiem tra script: doc to toan bo, dam bao mach lac, moi cau co ly do ton tai.

### Buoc 3 — Chon Visual Style va Nhac Nen
- Dinh nghia visual style: Minimal (nen trang/den, text lon), Dynamic (nhieu cut, B-roll),
  Lifestyle (canh thuc te, con nguoi), hoac Animation (motion graphic).
- Chon palette mau theo Brand Color Guide (neu co) hoac theo cam xuc muc tieu.
- Nhac nen: chon tu thu vien copyright-free (Epidemic Sound, Pixabay Music, YouTube Audio Library).
  Nhac phai match voi energy: upbeat cho entertaining, chill cho giao duc.
- Dam bao am luong nhac nen: -12dB so voi giong noi de khong lam chim loi.

### Buoc 4 — Lap Shot List Chi Tiet
- Moi canh quay la 1 dong trong shot list: [So canh] [Mo ta canh] [Do dai] [Goc quay] [Thiet bi].
  Vi du: "Shot 03 | Tay cam san pham, xoay chau cham | 3s | Close-up | Dien thoai dung doc".
- Nhom shot theo trinh tu script: Hook shots / Content shots / CTA shots.
- Danh dau shot nao co the thay the bang stock footage (B-roll) de tiet kiem thoi gian quay.
- Shot list phai du chi tiet de nguoi quay khac co the thuc hien ma khong can giai thich them.

### Buoc 5 — Cau Hinh Auto-Caption va Subtitle
- Dung tool: CapCut Auto-Caption, Descript, hoac Whisper AI de tao subtitle tu dong.
- Kiem tra do chinh xac: xem lai tung dong, sua loi phat am, ten rieng, thuat ngu chuyen nganh.
- Format subtitle: font Bold, mau vang hoac trang, co 40-50px, align center, nen toi phan.
- Them highlight tu khoa (in dam, doi mau) cho cac cum tu quan trong de tang retention.
- Xuat file .srt rieng de dung lai cho cac bien the dich ngon ngu sau nay.

### Buoc 6 — Xuat File Theo Spec Tung Nen Tang
- TikTok / Reels / Shorts: 1080x1920 (9:16), H.264, 30fps, bitrate >= 5Mbps, am thanh AAC 44.1kHz.
- YouTube (horizontal): 1920x1080 (16:9), H.264, 24/30fps, bitrate >= 8Mbps.
- LinkedIn / Twitter: 1080x1080 (1:1) hoac 1920x1080, MP4, toi da 140MB.
- Khong crop hay stretch; dung template xuat cho tung nen tang trong Premiere/DaVinci/CapCut.
- Dat ten file chuan: [Brand]_[Nen tang]_[Ngay]_[Version].mp4.

### Buoc 7 — Phan Phoi Da Kenh Va Theo Doi Hieu Qua
- Dung Buffer/Later/Metricool de schedule tung nen tang theo gio vang (xem SKILL_MED_21 Buoc 6).
- Dang nen tang chinh truoc 30 phut, cac nen tang phu sau.
- Sau 48h: chup man hinh analytics (views, watch time %, likes, comments, shares).
- Neu Watch Time < 40%, xem lai hook; neu Drop-off cao o giua, xem lai Content section.
- Ghi ket qua vao Video Performance Tracker de tich luy data cho cac dot sau.

## VI DU THUC TE

Tinh huong: Khoa hoc online "Hoc Python 30 ngay" can 4 video quang cao cho TikTok va Reels.

Buoc 1: Muc tieu = Ban hang. KPI = Click link bio, Dang ky khoa hoc. Do dai 30s moi video.
Buoc 2: Hook: "Python hoc 30 ngay nhung 90% nguoi bo cuoc o buoc nay." Content: 3 ly do bo cuoc
         + cach khoa hoc giai quyet. CTA: "Link dang ky trong bio, con 20 suat."
Buoc 3: Visual style Dynamic. Nhac Epidemic Sound - bai "Tech Upbeat". Am luong nhac -12dB.
Buoc 4: Shot list 8 canh: 2 hook shot (man hinh code mat + text overlay), 4 content shot,
         2 CTA shot (nguoi tro tay len man hinh dien thoai).
Buoc 5: Dung CapCut Auto-Caption, sua 3 loi nhan dang, highlight "30 ngay" va "Python" mau vang.
Buoc 6: Xuat TikTok 1080x1920, Reels 1080x1920, Stories 1080x1920 tu 1 file goc.
Buoc 7: Schedule TikTok luc 19:30, Reels luc 20:00. Sau 48h: Watch Time 62%, CTR 4.8% -> scale.
