## QUY TRINH CHI TIET

### BUOC 1 - RESEARCH: Doc vi vi nguoi doc (Buyer Persona)
- Xac dinh chan dung khach hang muc tieu: Ho la ai? Do tuoi? Nganh nghe?
- Tim hieu "Noi dau" (Pain Point) lon nhat cua ho la gi? (Ho dang lo lang, mat mat dieu gi?).
- Xac dinh "Mong muon tham kin" (Desire) cua ho (Ho muon dat duoc muc tieu nao?).
- Liet ke tat ca nhung diem ho con nghi ngo, phan doi (Objections) truoc khi mua hang.

### BUOC 2 - USP DEFINITION: Dinh vi gia tri doc nhat
- Xac dinh USP (Unique Selling Proposition) cua san pham/dich vu: Tai sao ho phai mua san pham nay chu khong phai cua doi thu?
- Chuyen hoa dac tinh san pham (Features) thanh loi ich thuc te cho khach hang (Benefits) theo cong thuc: "So What? -> Which means...".

### BUOC 3 - HOOK & HEADLINE: Che tao tieu de kho cuong
- Viet 10-15 bien the tieu de khac nhau de A/B test.
- Su dung cac cong thuc headline kinh dien:
  - Dua tren con so: "3 buoc don gian de..."
  - Dua tren su to mo: "Bi mat ma cac chuyen gia marketing giau ban..."
  - Dua tren noi dau: "Ban van dang mat 3 tieng moi ngay cho...?"
  - Huong dan cach lam: "Lam the nao de..."

### BUOC 4 - STRUCTURE: Áp dung framework thuyet phuc
Chon mot trong cac framework sau tuy theo format noi dung:
- PAS (Problem - Agitate - Solve): Neu van de -> Xoay sau vao noi dau -> Gioi thieu giai phap. (Phu hop cho FB Ads, Email ngan).
- AIDA (Attention - Interest - Desire - Action): Thu hut -> Gay thich thu -> Tao khao khat -> Keu goi hanh dong. (Phu hop cho Landing Page, VSL).
- BAB (Before - After - Bridge): Truoc day kho so -> Sau nay sung suong -> San pham la chiec cau noi.

### BUOC 5 - SOCIAL PROOF & TRUST: Xay dung long tin
- Chen bang chung thuc te: So lieu thong ke, ket qua nghien cuu.
- Dua vao cac feedback, testimonial, case study tu khach hang da su dung thanh cong (kem ten, hinh anh, ket qua cu the).
- Dat chung nhan, cam ket hoan tien (Guarantee) de triet tieu rui ro cho khach hang (Risk Reversal).

### BUOC 6 - CALL TO ACTION (CTA): Thiet ke loi keu goi hanh dong
- CTA phai ro rang, duy nhat va huong vao loi ich (vi du: "DANG KY NHAN UU DAI NGAY" thay vi "Gửi").
- Tao cam giac khan cap (Urgency) va khan hiem (Scarcity) that su: gioi han so luong nguoi dang ky hoac thoi gian khuyen mai.

### BUOC 7 - EDIT & POLISH: Chinh sua giong dieu thoi mien
- Loai bo cac tu ngu sa sao, chung chung (e.g., "tot nhat", "uy tin hang dau").
- Su dung cac dong tu manh, tu goi hinh, goi cam xuc.
- Kiem tra tinh troi chay (Readability): su dung doan van ngan, cau ngan, ngat dong de nguoi doc de doc luot (scan).
