## QUY TRINH CHI TIET

### BUOC 1 - TECHNICAL AUDIT: Quet va phan tich he thong
- Kiem tra toc do tai trang (Core Web Vitals): LCP, FID, CLS.
- Kiem tra crawlability va indexability (file robots.txt, sitemap.xml, loi 404, redirect 301).
- Ra soat phien ban Mobile-friendly va chung chi bao mat HTTPS/SSL.
- Su dung Google Search Console va Screaming Frog de thu thap tat ca cac loi ky thuat.

### BUOC 2 - KEYWORD RESEARCH: Nghien cuu tu khoa chien luoc
- Xac dinh bo tu khoa hat giong (Seed Keywords) phu hop voi san pham/dich vu.
- Tim kiem cac tu khoa duoi dai (Long-tail Keywords) co do canh tranh thap nhung ty le chuyen doi cao.
- Phan tich Search Intent (Y dinh tim kiem): Informational, Navigational, Commercial, Transactional.
- Lap bang phan nhom tu khoa dua tren volume search va muc do kho (KD - Keyword Difficulty).

### BUOC 3 - TOPIC CLUSTERING: Xay dung cau truc noi dung
- Thiet ke trang tru cot (Pillar Page) bao quat chu de rong.
- Xay dung cac bai viet ve tinh (Sub-pages / Cluster Pages) de giai thich sau tung khia canh.
- Lien ket chat che giua Pillar Page va Cluster Pages bang he thong internal link hai chieu de truyen Authority.
- Tranh hien tuong an thit tu khoa (Keyword Cannibalization).

### BUOC 4 - ON-PAGE OPTIMIZATION: Toi uu hoa tren trang
- Toi uu URL: ngan, chua tu khoa chinh, dung dau gach ngang (e.g., /seo-website-len-top).
- Viet Title Tag (<= 60 ky tu) va Meta Description (<= 160 ky tu) hap dan, chua tu khoa.
- Dat the H1 duy nhat cho tieu de bai viet, chia noi dung bang H2, H3 hop ly.
- Toi uu hinh anh: ten file khong dau, co the ALT mo ta hinh anh chua tu khoa, nen dung format WebP.

### BUOC 5 - CONTENT SEO (E-E-A-T): Sáng tạo noi dung chat luong
- Viet noi dung theo chuan E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness).
- Dam bao thong tin chinh xac, co nguon tham khao tu cac to chuc uy tin.
- Bo sung y kien chuyen gia, so lieu thuc te va case study thuc tien de tang diem doc doc quyen.
- Mat do tu khoa (Keyword Density) duy tri tu 1% - 2%, phan bo tu nhien o 150 tu dau va cuoi bai.

### BUOC 6 - OFF-PAGE & LINK BUILDING: Xay dung lien ket ngoai
- Tim kiem nguon backlink uy tin tu cac trang bao chi, blog cung nganh co chi so DA (Domain Authority) cao.
- Xay dung lien ket tu nhien qua Guest Posting, PR bao chi, hoac tao tai nguyen mien phi thu hut link tu nhien.
- Monitoring va loai bo (disavow) cac backlink ban, spam gay anh huong den uy tin website.

### BUOC 7 - SCHEMA & TRACKING: Cau hinh Schema va do luong
- Chen Schema Markup (Structured Data) phu hop: Article, Product, FAQ, LocalBusiness.
- Cau hinh Google Analytics 4 (GA4) va Google Search Console de theo doi cac chi so.
- Do luong va bao cao hang tuan: organic clicks, CTR, keyword rankings, conversion rate.
