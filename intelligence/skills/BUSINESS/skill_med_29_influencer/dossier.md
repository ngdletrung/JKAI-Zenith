## QUY TRINH CHI TIET

### BUOC 1 - TARGETING: Xac dinh tieu chi KOL phu hop
- Phan loai chien dich: Mass Awareness (Phuong phap phu rong) hay Niche Conversion (Chuyen doi ngach).
- Xac dinh tier cua KOL can tim kiem:
  - Mega (>= 1M followers).
  - Macro (100k - 1M followers).
  - Micro (10k - 100k followers).
  - Nano (1k - 10k followers).
- Dinh nghia Niche (Linh vuc) phu hop: Tech, Beauty, Lifestyle, Finance, Family.

### BUOC 2 - SCOUTING: Tim kiem va san loc ung vien
- Su dung cac tool thu thap du lieu: Social Blade, HypeAuditor, TikTok Creator Marketplace.
- Tim kiem thu cong qua Hashtag, doi thu canh tranh va cac group review nganh.
- Lap danh sach so bo (Longlist) gom 15-20 KOLs tiem nang.

### BUOC 3 - AUDITING: Tham dinh chat luong thuc te
Phan tich chi tiet cac thong so cua tung KOL:
- Engagement Rate (ER) - Ty le tuong tac: ER = (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Followers. ER chuan phai >= 3% tren TikTok/Instagram.
- Audience Authenticity: Kiem tra follower ao, bot, spam bang cach quet comment.
- Brand Safety: Ra soat lich su phat ngon, scandal hoac cac bai dang tieu cuc de tranh rui ro truyen thong.

### BUOC 4 - OUTREACH: Tiep can & Dam phan gia
- Soan thu pitch (outreach email/DM) ca nhan hoa, the hien su ton trong va neu ro ly do vi sao thuong hieu phu hop voi ho.
- Dam phan ve gia ca, quyen loi (PR post, review, livestream, affiliate commission) va cac cam ket ve KPI (luot xem, luot click, luot mua hang).
- Dat shortlist cuoi cung phu hop voi ngan sach du an.

### BUOC 5 - BRIEFING: Xay dung ban dac ta noi dung
Soan thao Brief hop tac chi tiet gui cho KOL:
- Gioi thieu san pham & Key Messages (Thong diep chinh).
- Yeu cau format: Video thoi luong bao lau? Post luc may gio? Tag nhung hashtag nao?
- Cac dieu cam ky (Don'ts): Khong nhac den ten doi thu, khong de hinh anh thieu sang, khong vi pham chinh sach platform.
- Quy dinh disclose: Yeu cau tag #AD hoac #Quảng_cáo theo quy dinh phap luat.

### BUOC 6 - CONTENT REVIEW: Kiem duyet noi dung truoc gio dang
- Yeu cau KOL gui ban demo truoc 24h - 48h truoc gio on-air.
- Kiem tra xem KOL co doc dung ten san pham, truyen tai dung key message, am thanh ro rang hay khong.
- Kiem duyet cac thong tin ky thuat (Link mua hang co dung UTM tracking code khong?).

### BUOC 7 - ANALYTICS & RELATIONSHIP: Bao cao hieu qua
- Do luong hieu qua sau 7 ngay: Reach, Views, Comments, Shares, Clicks on link, CPL (Cost Per Lead), CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost).
- Tinh toan EMV (Earned Media Value) cua chien dich.
- Luu tru va phan loai KOLs hoat dong hieu qua cao de xay dung quan he dai han (Brand Ambassador).
