## QUY TRINH CHI TIET

### BUOC 1 - NHAN DIEN: Xac dinh loai va tinh chat van ban
- Phan biet ro: Cong van di (gui ra ngoai), Cong van den (tiep nhan, xu ly), Bien ban hop,
  To trinh (xin phe duyet), Nghi quyet (quyet dinh tap the), Hop dong (rang buoc phap ly),
  Bao cao (dinh ky / dot xuat), Thong bao noi bo
- Xac dinh cap do van ban: cap Cong ty, cap Phong ban, cap Du an
- Xac dinh do khan: Khong khan / Khan / Thuong khan / Mat (anh huong den xu ly va duong chuyen)
- Neu thieu thong tin: hoi ro ten co quan phat hanh, ngay can hoan thanh, ky ten ai

### BUOC 2 - MAU CHUAN: Ap dung khung phap ly dung
- Van ban hanh chinh doi ngoai: Ap dung Nghi dinh 30/2020/ND-CP ve cong tac van thu
- Van ban noi bo cong ty: Ap dung mau da duoc Bo phan Hanh chinh ban hanh hoac mau JKAI
- Hop dong kinh te: Can kem dieu khoan theo Luat Thuong mai 2005 va Bo luat Dan su 2015
- Khong tu y thay doi ket cau mau chuan neu chua xin phe duyet - chi chinh noi dung can dien
- Luu y: mau co quan nha nuoc khac mau doanh nghiep, khong lan lon

### BUOC 3 - PHAN MUC: Soan dung thu tu theo the thuc van ban
- Quoc hieu - Tieu ngu: "CONG HOA XA HOI CHU NGHIA VIET NAM / Doc lap - Tu do - Hanh phuc"
  (can giua, in hoa, in dam)
- Ten co quan ban hanh: Ten day du, in hoa (goc trai tren)
- So hieu van ban: So thu tu / Ky hieu loai van ban / Nam phat hanh (vi du: 15/CV-JKAI-HC)
- Dia danh va ngay thang: Goc phai, dong voi So hieu (vi du: Ha Noi, ngay 18 thang 6 nam 2026)
- Trich yeu noi dung: In dam, nam giua, tom tat noi dung chinh trong 1 cau, khong qua 25 tu
- Phan noi dung chinh: Viet theo cac muc I, II, III hoac 1, 2, 3 tuy do dai
- Noi nhan: Linh vuc phu trach (de xu ly), luu VP, luu HS (de luu tru)
- Ky ten, dong dau: Chuc danh, ho ten nguoi ky, dong dau co quan neu can

### BUOC 4 - NOI DUNG: Viet chuan muc, chinh xac
- Dung van phong hanh chinh: ngan gon, chinh xac, khong da nghia, khong ca cam ca nhan
- Moi y mot doan, moi doan khong qua 5-6 dong, tranh dung cau phuc hon 3 menh de
- Dung thi hien tai va tuong lai, tranh thi qua khu (tru khi tuong trinh su viec da xay ra)
- Neu can cu phap ly: trich dan day du "Can cu Luat X, Dieu Y..." truoc phan quyet dinh
- Dan chung so lieu phai chinh xac va co nguon, khong dung so lieu uoc luong cho van ban phap ly
- Kiem tra lai: ai lam gi, den khi nao, ket qua can dat la gi - neu ro het 3 yeu to nay

### BUOC 5 - KIEM TRA PHAP LY: Ra soat truoc khi ky
- Kiem tra tham quyen ky: loai van ban nay ai duoc ky? (Giam doc / Pho GD / Truong phong?)
- Kiem tra can cu: van ban co doi lieu phap ly hop le khong? (nghi dinh, quyet dinh, dieu le cong ty)
- Doi chieu voi quy che noi bo: co vi pham chinh sach nhan su, tai chinh, quan tri khong?
- Neu hop dong: kiem tra cac dieu khoan giai quyet tranh chap, phat vi pham, bat kha khang
- Neu to trinh xin kinh phi: kem du luan chung, bao gia, de nghi phe duyet cu the
- Neu co rui ro phap ly: danh dau ro va bao cao cho cap quan ly truoc khi phat hanh

### BUOC 6 - DINH DANG: Tuan thu chuan trinh bay
- Font: Times New Roman, co chu 13pt cho noi dung chinh, 14pt cho tieu de chinh
- Le trang: Trai 3 cm, Phai 2 cm, Tren 2.5 cm, Duoi 2.5 cm (theo Nghi dinh 30/2020)
- Gian dong: 1.5 lines cho noi dung, khong them khoang cach thua giua cac doan
- Danh so trang tu trang 2 tro di, vi tri giua day trang, font dong voi noi dung
- Ky so: Su dung chu ky so (digital signature) neu van ban gui qua email chinh thuc
- Khong dung mau nen, khong dung watermark tru khi la tai lieu mat co quy dinh rieng

### BUOC 7 - LUU TRU: Quan ly ho so bai ban
- Ma hoa va dat ten file: [NamThang]-[SoHieu]-[TrichYeuNgan].pdf (vi du: 202606-15CV-JKAI-BaoCaoQ2.pdf)
- Danh muc theo he thong thu muc: /Van-ban-di/, /Van-ban-den/, /Hop-dong/, /Bao-cao/, /Bien-ban/
- Luu toi thieu 2 ban: 1 ban goc co chu ky (scan PDF), 1 ban chinh sua duoc (.docx)
- Giao van ban dung nguoi: xac nhan qua email hoac he thong noi bo, co timestamp nhan
- Set nhac lich: nhac han xu ly, han hieu luc hop dong, han bao cao dinh ky
- Tieu huy dung quy trinh khi het hieu luc (van ban mat can huy theo quy trinh bao mat)
