## QUY TRINH CHI TIET

### BUOC 1 - AUDIENCE SEGMENTATION: Phan nhom khach hang
- Lay danh sach nguoi nhan va phan loai theo hanh vi (moi dang ky, da mua hang, khach hang VIP, giỏ hang bi bo quen).
- Xac dinh cac truong thong tin ca nhan hoa (Merge Tags) nhu: Ten, Cong ty, Nhu cau, Linh vuc.
- Xoa bo cac dia chi email loi, email khong con hoat dong de bao ve uy tin domain (Sender Reputation).

### BUOC 2 - FUNNEL MAPPING: Thiet ke lo trinh chuoi email
- Dinh nghia so luong email trong chuoi (vi du: chuoi Welcome gom 3 email gui cach nhau 24h - 48h).
- Xac dinh muc tieu cu the cua tung email (Email 1: Chao mung & Tang qua, Email 2: Chia se gia tri, Email 3: CTA ban hang/dat lich).
- Thiet lap cac dieu kien trigger logic (vi du: Neu nguoi dung mo Email 1 thi gui Email 2, neu khong mo thi gui lai kem headline khac).

### BUOC 3 - COPYWRITING: Viet noi dung email thoi mien
- Subject Line (Tieu de): Ngan gon (<= 50 ky tu), tao su to mo, khan cap hoac mang lai gia tri ro rang. Viet A/B test 2 phien ban.
- Body Copy (Noi dung chinh): Viet kieu ca nhan hoa nhu mot nguoi ban viet cho mot nguoi ban. Dat gia tri chia se len hang dau (Value-First).
- Call To Action (CTA): Chi dat 1 CTA duy nhat trong email, su dung link hoac button bat mat, dan den trang dich (Landing Page).
- Preheader Text: Doan text ngan hien thi ngay sau tieu de o hop thu den, bo tro cho tieu de de tang Open Rate.

### BUOC 4 - DESIGN & TEMPLATE: Thiet ke giao dien responsive
- Su dung layout don gian (Plain-text hoac HTML nhe) de tranh bi Gmail cho vao tab Promotions/Spam.
- Dam bao responsive 100% tren thiet bi di dong, font chu de doc (Arial, Helvetica tu 14-16pt).
- Chen anh co the Alt Day du va giam dung luong duoi 50KB de mail load nhanh nhat.

### BUOC 5 - TECH CONFIGURATION: Cau hinh ky thuat gui thu
- Dam bao ten mien gui da duoc xac thuc day du cac ban ghi: SPF, DKIM, DMARC.
- Cai dat dia chi email gui dung ten thuong hieu (e.g., support@zenith.com thay vi dung gmail.com).
- Thiet lap thoi gian gui hop ly (vi du: 8am - 10am gio dia phuong cua nguoi nhan) de tang ty le tiep can.

### BUOC 6 - A/B TESTING: Thu nghiem va toi uu hoa
- Chay test A/B tieu de (Subject Line) tren 10% danh sach trong 2-4 gio de chon ra tieu de chien thang.
- Thu nghiem phan biet thoi gian gui (Gui sang vs Gui chieu) hoac ten nguoi gui (e.g., "Zenith Support" vs "LeeTrung tu Zenith").

### BUOC 7 - ANALYTICS: Phao sat hieu qua & Clean List
- Do luong cac chi so sau 24h - 48h: Open Rate (Muc tieu >= 20%), CTR (Muc tieu >= 2.5%), Bounce Rate (<= 2%), Unsubscribe Rate (<= 0.5%).
- Tu dong xoa hoac tag nhung lien he khong hoat dong (Unengaged Contacts) sau 90 ngay de duy ti suc khoe hop thu.

## CHECKLIST NHANH

- [ ] Danh sach khach hang da duoc loc sach va phan nhom phu hop.
- [ ] SPF, DKIM, DMARC cua domain gui da pass 100%.
- [ ] Subject Line va Preheader bat mat, khong vi pham cac tu khoa spam (e.g., FREE, SALE NOW, $$$).
- [ ] Email chi co duy nhat 1 Call To Action (CTA) ro rang.
- [ ] Da test gui thu ve hop thu cá nhân de kiem tra hien thi tren ca Mobile va Desktop.
- [ ] Link unsubscribe duoc dat o cuoi email theo dung luat bao mat.
