## KICH HOAT KHI

- Tai khoan mang xa hoi tang truong cham (duoi 5% follower/thang) hoac bi tren nguong trong nhieu thang.
- Engagement Rate (ER) duoi 2% tren Facebook hoac duoi 3% tren Instagram.
- Brand chua co chien luoc content ro rang, dang the gi tuy hung, khong co lich.
- Muon chuyen doi tu "nhieu follower ao" sang "cong dong chat luong mua hang".
- Chuan bi cho chien dich lon (ra mat san pham, su kien) can nen tang social manh.

## QUY TRINH THUC THI (7 BUOC)

### Buoc 1 — Social Media Audit Hien Trang
- Thu thap du lieu 90 ngay gan nhat: Follower growth rate, Reach, Impressions,
  Engagement Rate, Top 10 posts (theo reach va ER), Post frequency.
- Phan tich Audience Insights: do tuoi, gioi tinh, dia ly, gio hoat dong cao diem.
- So sanh KPI voi benchmark nganh: ER Facebook <1% (kem), 1-3% (trung binh), >3% (tot).
- Liet ke 5 bai dang dat ket qua tot nhat va 5 bai dat ket qua kem nhat, tim pattern.

### Buoc 2 — Nghien Cuu Doi Thu (Competitive Intelligence)
- Chon 3-5 tai khoan doi thu hoac nguoi dan dau nganh de theo doi.
- Phan tich: content type (video/image/text), tan suat dang, hashtag su dung, cach xu ly comment.
- Xac dinh "content gap": chu de ho chua lam tot ma audience quan tam -> day la co hoi.
- Dung Metricool, Fanpage Karma, hoac SimilarWeb de lay so lieu doi thu.
- Ket qua: ma tran SWOT social media cua brand so voi thi truong.

### Buoc 3 — Xac Dinh Niche va Content Pillars
- Chon 1 niche cu the (khong qua rong): vi du "Suc khoe cho nguoi di lam ban ron" thay vi "Suc khoe".
- Xay dung 3-5 Content Pillars (tru cot noi dung): moi pillar la mot chu de chinh ma brand se noi.
  Vi du: (1) Kien thuc chuyen mon, (2) Cau chuyen khach hang, (3) Hau truong thuong hieu,
  (4) San pham/dich vu, (5) Tin tuc nganh.
- Ti le phan bo: 60% giao duc/giai tri, 30% community/tuong tac, 10% quang cao truc tiep.
- Moi pillar phai co it nhat 20 y tuong noi dung (brainstorm truoc khi bat dau thang).

### Buoc 4 — Lap Content Calendar 30 Ngay
- Lap bang lich theo cac cot: Ngay | Nen tang | Pillar | Format | Chu de | Trang thai.
- Tan suat toi thieu theo nen tang: TikTok/Reels 5-7 post/tuan; Facebook/Instagram 3-5 post/tuan;
  LinkedIn 2-3 post/tuan; Twitter/X 1-3 post/ngay.
- Xen ke format: khong dang 3 video lien tiep, khong dang 3 anh lien tiep; can variety.
- Danh dau cac ngay dac biet (su kien, ngay le, ra mat san pham) de tao noi dung themed.
- Hoan thanh calendar truoc ngay 25 cua thang, buffer 3-5 bai du tru cho tinh huong khan cap.

### Buoc 5 — Community Building (Xay Dung Cong Dong)
- Reply moi comment trong 1h dau sau khi dang (thoi diem vang de day engagement).
  Reply phai: ca nhan hoa, co cau hoi tro lai hoac tao cuoc hoi thoai, khong dung bot template.
- DM outreach: gui tin nhan ca nhan hoa cho follower moi hoac nguoi like/share noi dung.
  Khong spam; muc dich la chuyen hoa tu fan -> khach hang -> brand advocate.
- Collaboration: collab voi 2-4 creator cung niche moi thang (IG Collab post, TikTok Duet, Stitch).
- Tao community ritual: quiz hang tuan, Q&A thu Sau, "Cau chuyen cua ban" thu Hai.

### Buoc 6 — Paid Amplification Khi Organic Dat Nguong
- Dieu kien kich hoat paid: organic reach giam xuong duoi 5% total page likes, hoac
  co bai dat ER cao (>5%) can scale nhanh.
- Chay Dark Post hoac Boost bai dat hieu qua cao nhat (top 10% ER), ngay sach nhat.
- Budget khoi dau: 200.000-500.000 VND/ngay, target Lookalike Audience tu custom audience.
- A/B test audience: Lookalike 1% vs Lookalike 3% vs Interest-based targeting.
- Stop-loss rule: dung chay neu CPE (cost per engagement) vuot 3x so voi target sau 48h.

### Buoc 7 — Bao Cao KPI Tuan va Thang
- Bao cao tuan (moi thu Hai): Reach, Impression, ER, Follower +/-, Top post, Anomaly.
- Bao cao thang (ngay cuoi thang): So sanh thang truoc, tien do muc tieu quy, de xuat dieu chinh.
- Dashboard: dung Metricool hoac Google Looker Studio de tu dong hoa bao cao.
- Khi ER giam >20% so voi tuan truoc: phan tich nguyen nhan trong 24h va hieu chinh ngay.
