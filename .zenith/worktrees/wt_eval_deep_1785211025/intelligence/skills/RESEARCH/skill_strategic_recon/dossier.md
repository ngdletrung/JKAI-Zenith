## QUY TRINH CHI TIET

### BUOC 1 - MISSION BRIEF: Xac dinh muc tieu trinh sat
- Nhan dien muc tieu chien dich: Doi thu (Competitor), Thi truong (Market), Cong nghe (Tech), hay Khach hang (Customer).
- Liệt ke cac cau hoi can tra loi (vi du: Doanh thu cua doi thu X nam 2025 la bao nhieu? Ho dung tech stack gi?).
- Xac dinh pham vi dia ly va khoang thoi gian khao sat.

### BUOC 2 - SOURCE MAPPING: Lap ban do nguon tin
- Liet ke cac nguon tin can khai thac:
  - Surface Web: Google Search, News, Blogs, Cong thong tin doanh nghiep.
  - Deep Web (hop phap): So ke hoach dau tu, co so du lieu bang sang che (Google Patents), tin tuyen dung (Indeed, Glassdoor).
  - Social & Community: Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn, Twitter, GitHub.
- Thiet lap tu khoa tim kiem nang cao (Search Operators: site:, filetype:, intitle:).

### BUOC 3 - DEEP SEARCH: Tien hanh quet du lieu da tang
- Chay cac vong lap quet tin tuong tac (Recursive Search) de tim kiem tu khoa tu rong den sau.
- Dung trinh duyet ảo de chui vao cac trang web, chup screenshot hoac cao HTML cua cac forum, cong dong.
- Luu tru tat ca cac duong link nguon (Sources) de lam bang chung doi chieu.

### BUOC 4 - SIGNAL DETECTION: Phat hien tin hieu yeu (Weak Signals)
- Kiem tra cac tin tuyen dung gan day cua doi thu de suy doan huong di san pham moi (vi du: ho dang tuyen rat nhieu AI Engineer).
- Kiem tra dang ky nhan hieu (Trademarks) hoac bang sang che moi cua ho.
- Doc cac feedback, review tieu cuc tu khach hang cua doi thu de tim ra "khoang trong co hoi" (Opportunity Gaps).

### BUOC 5 - COMPETITIVE MATRIX: Xay dung ma tran so sanh
- Tong hop so lieu va lap ma tran so sanh giua doanh nghiep minh va top 3-5 doi thu hang dau.
- Cac tieu chi so sanh: Gia ca (Pricing), Tinh nang (Features), Tốc do (Performance), Ho tro khach hang (Customer Support), Thi phan (Market Share).

### BUOC 6 - SYNTHESIS REPORT: Bien tap bao cao tinh bao
- Ap dung tu duy tong hop (Synthesis) tu du lieu tho sang Insights va Implications (Y nghia chien luoc).
- Trinh bay bao cao theo khung:
  - Executive Summary (BLUF).
  - Market Overview & Key Trends.
  - Competitor Deep-Dive.
  - SWOT Analysis & Strategic Recommendations.
- Luon phan biet ro giua Fact (Su that co bang chung) va Assumption (Gia dinh/Suy doan).

### BUOC 7 - MONITORING SETUP: Cai dat giam sat lien tuc
- Setup he thong tu dong theo doi tu khoa qua Google Alerts hoac RSS Feeds.
- Cai dat cron job quet website doi thu định ky de phat hien thay doi pricing hoac landing page.
- Cap nhat dinh ky bao cao tinh bao hang thang/quy.
