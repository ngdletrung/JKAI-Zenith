## QUY TRINH CHI TIET

### BUOC 1 - GOAL & CONCEPT: Xac dinh muc tieu & Kich ban tong the
- Xac dinh muc tieu su kien: Lead Generation (thu lead), Branding (thuong hieu), Sales (ban hang) hay Educating (dao tao).
- Chon chu de (Topic) bat mat, giai quyet mot van de nong hoi cua nguoi nghe.
- Chon platform phu hop: Zoom, Google Meet, Hopin, Microsoft Teams, StreamYard, Youtube Live.
- Phac thao thoi luong chuong trinh (e.g., 60 phut, 90 phut, 120 phut).

### BUOC 2 - PROMOTION: Thiet ke he thong dang ky & Quang ba
- Tao Landing Page dang ky su kien voi form thu thap thong tin (Ten, Email, So dien thoai, Cau hoi cho dien gia).
- Viet copy cho trang dang ky lam noi bat loi ich khi tham gia su kien va thong tin dien gia.
- Chay cac chien dich promotion: gui email cho danh sach san co, dang bai tren mang xa hoi, collab voi doi tac.

### BUOC 3 - EMAIL REMINDER SEQUENCE: He thong mail nhac lich
Thiet lap chuoi email tu dong gui den nguoi dang ky de giu nhiet va tang show-up rate (ty le tham gia thuc te):
- Email 1: Xac nhan dang ky thanh cong + Link tham gia (Gui ngay lap tuc).
- Email 2: Nhac nho truoc 1 ngay + Tai lieu doc truoc (neu co).
- Email 3: Nhac nho truoc 1 tieng + Day la co hoi cuoi cung.
- Email 4: Nhac nho truoc 15 phut + Phong cho da mo.

### BUOC 5 - LIVE EXECUTION & INTERACTION: Van hanh live & Tuong tac
- On-air dung gio quy dinh, bat dau bang nhac nen hoac video intro nhe de chao mung nguoi nghe.
- Theo doi sat Agenda, ho tro MC va Speakers chuyen slide hoac dieu phoi camera.
- Tang tuong tac: Tao cac phien Q&A, thuc hien khao sat nhanh (Live Polls), to chuc mini-game tang qua o cuoi chuong trinh.
- Theo doi khung chat cua nguoi xem, an bot cac comment tieu cuc, spam hoac link nhiem doc.

### BUOC 6 - POST-EVENT FOLLOW-UP: Cham soc sau su kien
- Gui email cam on ngay sau khi ket thuc su kien 2-4 tieng.
- Email bao gom: Link xem lai (Replay link), Slide bai thuyet trinh, Link khao sat y kien hoac form dang ky tu van ban hang.
- Chuyen cac lead nong cho bo phan sales follow-up kip thoi.

### BUOC 7 - ANALYTICS & ROI: Do luong va danh gia
- Do luong cac chi so quan trong:
  - Registrants (So nguoi dang ky).
  - Attendees (So nguoi tham gia thuc te) & Show-up Rate (Attendees / Registrants).
  - Average Watch Time (Thoi gian xem trung binh) de danh gia do hap dan cua noi dung.
  - Conversion Rate (Ty le dang ky tu van, mua hang cuoi su kien).
- Hop rut kinh nghiem (Post-mortem) de toi uu cho cac su kien tiep theo.
