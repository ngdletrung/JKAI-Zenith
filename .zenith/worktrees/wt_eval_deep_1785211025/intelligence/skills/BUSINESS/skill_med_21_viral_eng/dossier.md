## QUY TRINH THUC THI (7 BUOC)

### Buoc 1 — Phan tich xu huong hien tai (Trend Intelligence)
- Quet Google Trends, TikTok Creative Center, Twitter/X Trending moi ngay luc 7:00.
- Loc trending topics theo nganh hang cua brand (dung bo loc category).
- Danh gia vong doi xu huong: "rising" (bam ngay) vs "peak" (can bien the) vs "declining" (bo qua).
- Ket qua: danh sach 3-5 xu huong phu hop, kem du lieu search volume va do tuoi.

### Buoc 2 — Xac dinh Hook 3 Giay Dau Tien
- Video: frame dau phai co visual shock, pattern interrupt, hoac cau hoi gay to mo.
- Text/caption: chu dau tien phai la so, cam xuc manh, hoac mot tuyen bo bat ngo.
- Kiem tra hook bang cong thuc: TUYEN BO MANH + SU KY LA + LOI HUA GIA TRI ro rang.
- Viet toi thieu 5 bien the hook, chon 2 bien the tot nhat de thu nghiem.

### Buoc 3 — Viet Headline va Caption Toi Uu Cam Xuc
- AP dung cac driver tam ly: FOMO (so bo lo), Curiosity Gap (khoang trong tri thuc),
  Social Proof (nguoi khac dang lam), Urgency (thoi han), va Identity Trigger (toi la ai).
- Caption structure: Hook (1-2 dong) -> Gia tri chinh (3-5 dong) -> Cam xuc/cau chuyen (2-3 dong) -> CTA.
- Hashtag: 3-5 hashtag chinh xac (khong spam); 1 hashtag niche, 1 hashtag trending, 1 hashtag brand.
- Kiem tra doc lai toan bo: ton tai 1 "dan du" chuyen sang slide/link tiep theo hay khong.

### Buoc 4 — Tao Bien The A/B (2-3 Phien Ban)
- Bien the A: hook dua tren cam xuc (gia tri, cam hung, noi dau).
- Bien the B: hook dua tren so lieu (so cu the, ket qua do luong duoc).
- Bien the C (neu can): hook dua tren tranh luan hoac quan diem trai chieu.
- Moi bien the thay doi toi da 1-2 yeu to (hook hoac format), giu nguyen CTA de do chinh xac.

### Buoc 5 — Chon Format Phu Hop Voi Nen Tang
- Short Video (15-60s): phu hop giao duc nhanh, how-to, entertainment.
- Carousel (5-10 slide): phu hop list, so sanh, step-by-step guide.
- Thread (Twitter/X): phu hop goc nhin chuyen gia, chuoi suy luan, chuyen ke.
- Single image + long caption: phu hop cau chuyen ca nhan, tin tuc, thong bao.
- Quyet dinh format dua tren: noi dung cua brand, hanh vi audience, format dang duoc platform uu tien.

### Buoc 6 — Lap Lich Dang Theo Gio Vang VN
- Khung gio sang: 06:30 - 09:00 (nguoi dung kiem tra dien thoai khi thuc day).
- Khung gio trua: 11:30 - 13:00 (gio nghi an trua).
- Khung gio toi: 19:00 - 21:30 (sau gio lam viec, truoc khi ngu).
- Cuoi tuan: them khung 09:00 - 11:00 Saturday, Sunday.
- Dung cong cu Buffer/Later/Meta Business Suite de schedule tu dong.

### Buoc 7 — Do Luong va Toi Uu Lien Tuc
- Theo doi sau 1h dang: neu CTR video duoi 5%, xem xet thay thumbnail.
- Sau 24h: so sanh bien the A/B theo Reach, Engagement Rate, Share, Save.
- Sau 7 ngay: tong hop bao cao, xac dinh pattern noi dung nao hoat dong tot.
- Nhap ket qua vao Content Performance Log de huan luyen pattern cho cac dot tiep theo.
