## QUY TRINH 7 BUOC

### Buoc 1 — BRIEF: Xac dinh doi tuong va muc tieu
- Ai la nguoi doc? (CEO / HDQT / NHA DAU TU / BO TRUONG)
- Quyet dinh nao ho can dua ra sau khi doc?
- Han nop va format mong muon (1 trang / 5 trang / slide)?
- Giong dieu: chinh thuc / ban be / thuyet phuc?

### Buoc 2 — DATA HARVEST: Thu thap nguon luc
- Ket qua thuc hien: KPI, so lieu, milestone dat duoc
- So sanh voi ke hoach: thuc hien / ke hoach (%)
- Nguyen nhan cham / tre (neu co)
- Thanh tuu noi bat (co the chung minh bang so lieu)

### Buoc 3 — STRUCTURE: Xay dung khung BLUF
```
[KETS LUAN CHINH] — 2-3 cau, neu ngay van de + ket qua + de xuat
[BOI CANH] — 1 doan, tai sao can bao cao nay
[PHAN TICH] — Du lieu, bang bieu, so sanh
[RUI RO] — Top 3 rui ro va bien phap giam thieu
[DE XUAT] — Hanh dong cu the, nguoi chiu trach nhiem, deadline
```

### Buoc 4 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (toi da 1 trang A4)
- Cau 1: Ket qua tong the (dat / chua dat / vuot ke hoach)
- Cau 2-3: 3 thanh tich quan trong nhat (co so lieu)
- Cau 4-5: Van de lon nhat can giai quyet
- Cau 6: De xuat hanh dong uu tien
- Cau 7: Nguon luc / ngan sach can thiet (neu co)

### Buoc 5 — RISK AUDIT: Phan tich rui ro
Voi moi rui ro, trinh bay:
| Rui ro | Xac suat | Muc do anh huong | Bien phap giam thieu |
Tap trung top 3 rui ro cao nhat. Khong che giau rui ro.

### Buoc 6 — ACTION PLAN: De xuat hanh dong
- Moi de xuat = 1 dong: [HANH DONG] | [NGUOI CHIU TRACH NHIEM] | [DEADLINE] | [NGUON LUC]
- Sap xep theo do uu tien (P1/P2/P3)
- P1: Can quyet dinh ngay hom nay / tuan nay
- P2: Can quyet dinh trong thang nay
- P3: Co the cho den quy sau

### Buoc 7 — POLISH: Kiem tra cuoi cung
- Loai bo tat ca jargon kho hieu voi nguoi ngoai chuyen mon
- Moi bang bieu phai co chu thich ro rang
- Tat ca so lieu phai co nguon tham chieu
- Tong chieu dai: Executive Summary <= 1 trang, Full Report <= 5 trang
- Doc lai voi goc nhin: "Neu toi la CEO, toi co hieu khong?"
