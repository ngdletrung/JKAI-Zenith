## QUY TRINH CHI TIET

### BUOC 1 - BRIEF: Xac dinh tham so dau vao
- Hoi va ghi lai: Muc tieu trinh bay la gi? (thuyet phuc / bao cao ket qua / giao duc / ban hang)
- Xac dinh thoi luong: 5 phut (3-5 slide), 15 phut (8-12 slide), 30 phut (15-20 slide)
- Xac dinh doi tuong: NHA DAU TU (tap trung ROI, rui ro, thi truong), LANH DAO (ket qua, chien luoc,
  so lieu), KHACH HANG (loi ich, gia tri, cam xuc), KY THUAT (chi tiet trien khai, architecture)
- Xac dinh format dau ra: PowerPoint, Google Slides, PDF, hay Markdown reveal.js
- Ghi nhan ton giao dien neu co (logo, mau chinh, font cong ty)

### BUOC 2 - STRUCTURE: Ap dung MECE de xay dung khung slide
- Toi da 7 slide chinh cho deck 15 phut, moi slide bao giam 1 luan diem duy nhat
- Kiem tra MECE: cac y chinh khong chong cheo (Mutually Exclusive) va phu het noi dung (Collectively Exhaustive)
- Sap xep theo cau truc: Van de -> Giai phap -> Bang chung -> Hanh dong
- Xac dinh slide nao can bieu do, anh, bang, icon, so lieu lon (hero number)
- Danh so priority: slide nao co the cat neu bi giam thoi gian

### BUOC 3 - SLIDE FLOW: Xay dung luong truyen thong chuan
- Cover: Ten de tai, ten nguoi trinh bay, ngay, logo - khong qua 10 tu tieu de
- Pain / Opportunity: Neu van de bang so lieu thuc hoac kich ban cu the, 1 con so an tuong duy nhat
- Solution: Trinh bay giai phap 3-5 dong ngan gon, kem so sanh truoc/sau neu co
- Evidence / Demo: Dan chung case study, screenshot, KPI da dat duoc, testimonial co ten
- Team: Anh chan dung + 1 dong chuc danh + 1 thanh tuu noi bat moi nguoi
- Financials: Revenue model, unit economics, projection 3 nam, break-even point
- Ask / CTA: Yeu cau cu the (so tien, co phan, hop tac), buoc tiep theo ro rang va co deadline

### BUOC 4 - VISUAL: Tuan thu nguyen tac 1-1-30
- Moi slide: dung 1 y chinh duy nhat, 1 hinh anh hoac bieu do phu hop, khong qua 30 tu noi dung
- Tieu de slide phai la mot ket luan (vi du: "Doanh thu Q3 tang 40% so voi nam ngoai"), khong phai
  chu de chung chung (vi du: "Doanh thu Q3")
- Su dung khong gian trang (white space) de tang trong tam, khong nhet nhieu bullet points
- Chon palette 2-3 mau chinh, dat do tuong phan toi thieu 4.5:1 cho text tren nen
- Tat ca hinh anh phai co do phan giai >= 1920px, khong dung hinh clip art houc stock co watermark

### BUOC 5 - DATA VIZ: Chon dung loai bieu do
- Bar chart (cot dung/ngang): So sanh gia tri giua cac nhom (vi du: doanh thu tung quy)
- Line chart: The hien xu huong theo thoi gian (vi du: tang truong nguoi dung 12 thang)
- Pie / Donut: Ty le phan tram cua toan bo (chi dung khi <= 5 phan manh)
- Scatter plot: Tuong quan giua 2 bien so (vi du: chi phi marketing vs doanh thu)
- Waterfall: Phan tich tang giam tich luy (vi du: P&L breakdown)
- Heat map: Mat do hoac cuong do theo 2 chieu (vi du: hieu suat nhan vien x phong ban)
- Neu co bang so lieu tho, chuyen sang bieu do tuong ung, xoa tat ca so lieu thua

### BUOC 6 - STORYTELLING: Xay dung narrative arc
- Act 1 - Villain (Van de): Neu thuc trang dang gay dau don, mat mat, co hoi bi bo lo
- Act 2 - Hero (Giai phap): Gioi thieu giai phap nhu nhan vat hung, co kha nang giai quyet van de
- Act 3 - Victory (Ket qua): Chung minh ket qua da dat hoac du phong, kem con duong de dat duoc
- Moi slide phai phuc vu narrative arc, neu khong phai - cat hoac dua vao Appendix
- Dan cau chuyen bang so lieu that, ten nguoi that, dia diem that de tang tin nhiem

### BUOC 7 - DELIVERY: Hoan thien goi trinh bay
- Viet speaker notes moi slide: 3-5 cau, gom diem chinh can noi va cau chuyen bo sung
- Ghi thoi gian du kien moi slide (vi du: [2 phut]) vao dau speaker notes
- Tao 3-5 slide du phong (Appendix) cho Q&A: chi tiet ky thuat, bien phap, du lieu phu
- Kiem tra: khong co loi chinh ta, khong co slide trong, tat ca link va anh hien thi dung
- Export theo 2 format: file goc (.pptx hoac .key) va PDF de phong backup

## VI DU THUC TE

Yeu cau: "Lam pitch deck keu goi 2 ty VND cho startup EdTech, gap nha dau tu 20 phut"

Xu ly:
- Brief: Muc tieu thuyet phuc dau tu, 20 phut = 10 slide chinh + 3 Appendix, doi tuong NHA DAU TU
- Structure: Problem (HS thieu gia su) / Solution (App AI 1-on-1) / Market (12 ty USD) /
  Traction (500 users, 4.8 sao) / Model (subscription 199k/thang) / Team / Ask (2 ty, 15%)
- Visual: Slide Market dung pie chart thi phan, Traction dung line chart tang truong MAU
- Storytelling: Villain = hoc sinh truot thi vi thieu huong dan, Hero = AI tutor 24/7, Victory = 90%
  nguoi dung tang diem sau 30 ngay
- Delivery: Speaker notes ghi "60 giay" cho slide Cover, "3 phut" cho slide Market
